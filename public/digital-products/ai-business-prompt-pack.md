Claude is often better for long proposals. ChatGPT is often faster for short ads and variants.

## 1. Proposals & scope

**One-page proposal**
You are a senior freelance web consultant. Write a one-page proposal for [CLIENT TYPE] who needs [OUTCOME]. Budget range [X]. Include: problem, deliverables, timeline, investment, out of scope, next step. Tone: confident, clear, no fluff.

**Scope boundary**
List what is IN scope and OUT of scope for a [PROJECT TYPE] priced at [PRICE]. Protect me from unlimited revisions.

**Objection: too expensive**
Client said the quote is too high for [PROJECT]. Write 5 reply options that trade scope, not random discounts.

## 2. WhatsApp sales

**Greeting + menu**
Write a WhatsApp Business greeting and number menu for [BUSINESS] in [CITY]. Options: prices, book, location, human. Max 8 lines.

**Follow-up day 2**
Customer asked price for [SERVICE] then went quiet. Write a polite Day-2 follow-up under 4 lines.

**Away message**
Away message that still captures name, need, and preferred time for [BUSINESS].

## 3. Ads & social

**10 captions**
Write 10 Instagram/Facebook captions for [OFFER] aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Mix proof, education, and CTA. Each under 400 characters.

**Google headline set**
15 Google Responsive Search Ad headlines (30 chars max) and 4 descriptions (90 chars) for [SERVICE] in [LOCATION].

## 4. Website copy

**Home hero**
Write homepage hero headline, subheadline, and two CTA labels for [BUSINESS] that sells [RESULT] not features.

**Services section**
3 service cards: title, 2-line benefit, CTA, for [BUSINESS].

## 5. Email

Polite invoice follow-up for [AMOUNT] due [DATE], project [NAME]. Firm but professional.

**Cold outreach**
Cold email to [BUSINESS TYPE] whose website [PROBLEM]. Offer a free 3-minute audit. Under 120 words.
