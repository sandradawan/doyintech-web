# AI Content Engine Kit (30 days) — DoyinTech

1. List 10 customer questions.
2. Generate 30 caption angles (3 per question).
3. Shoot or design simple creatives.
4. Schedule Status / posts.

## Caption formula
Problem → one tip → soft CTA (reply YES / link).

## Status sequence
Frame 1 hook · Frame 2 tip · Frame 3 CTA.

## Weekly rhythm
Mon education · Wed proof · Fri offer · Sun soft CTA.
