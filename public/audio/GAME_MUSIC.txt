Signal Runner music
===================
Default: procedural ambient loop (Web Audio) — works with no file.

To use your own track:
1. Export an MP3 (loop-friendly, ~1–3 min, moderate volume).
2. Upload it to this folder as: public/audio/game-bg.mp3
3. Redeploy. The game will prefer that file when music is On.

Path must be exactly:
  public/audio/game-bg.mp3
