Independence Day splash audio
=============================

Put a licensed recording of the national anthem here:

  public/audio/nigeria-anthem.mp3

The splash page plays this file for ~10 seconds when it opens.

IMPORTANT
- Use only a recording you have the right to stream on your website.
- Do not upload pirated copies from YouTube or random downloads.
- Prefer an official or properly licensed instrumental/vocal version.
- Keep the file reasonably small (e.g. under 3 MB) for mobile users.

Browsers often block autoplay with sound. Visitors can tap
"Tap for national anthem" on the splash if audio does not start alone.
