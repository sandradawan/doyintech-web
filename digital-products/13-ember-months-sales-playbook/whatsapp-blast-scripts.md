# WhatsApp scripts for Ember 2026

Replace [ITEM], [PRICE], [DEPOSIT], [DATE]. Keep broadcasts short.

## Status hooks
1. Ember restock is not infinite. [ITEM] — [PRICE]. Deposit [DEPOSIT] today.
2. If you wait till 15 Dec, I cannot promise 20 Dec delivery. Lock now.
3. Two left in this colour. First transfer proof gets it.
4. Corporate hampers close [DATE]. Send company name + qty.
5. BF list goes out Friday 7am to people who replied BF.

## First reply when they say “how much?”
“[ITEM] Ember price is [PRICE] (list [LIST]). [DEPOSIT] deposit to lock. Balance before dispatch. Paystack or transfer. I send account + receipt now?”

## Deposit chase
“I can hold [ITEM] until 6pm today. After that it goes back to the list — Ember moves fast.”

## After they pay
“Seen. Packed in the [DATE] window. I will send rider name / tracking here. If anything changes I message you first.”

## Last-price without killing margin
“I can do [FLOOR PRICE] if you take it today and pay now. I cannot add free delivery on that rate.”

## Broadcast (opt-in only)
“Ember week: 3 items only.
1) [A] [PRICE]
2) [B] [PRICE]
3) Bundle [C] [PRICE]
Reply 1, 2 or 3 + your area. First 10 deposits this morning get Friday dispatch.”

## Angry delay
“You are right to ask. Rider slipped to [NEW DATE]. I can refund the delivery fee or add [SMALL GIFT]. Which do you prefer?”
