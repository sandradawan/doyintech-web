# Offer math that does not bankrupt you

## Rule
Never discount below: landed cost + packing + delivery cushion + 15% floor.

## Worked example (fashion / gadgets style)
- Landed cost: ₦8,000
- Packing + POS/transfer fuss: ₦400
- Delivery you might eat on complaints: ₦800
- Floor (15%): ₦1,380
- **Minimum selling price: ₦10,580**
- List price you show first: ₦13,500
- “Ember price”: ₦11,500 (still above floor)
- Bundle: item + add-on that costs you ₦1,200, sold together at ₦13,900

## Better than a flat % off
- Gift-with-purchase under ₦800 cost
- Free Lagos Island/Mainland delivery above a threshold you can afford
- Buy 2, get a small item (not buy 1 get 1)
- Early-bird deposit lock (price held until 15 Nov)

## What to write on Status
Bad: “50% off everything.”
Better: “Ember price ₦11,500 (was ₦13,500). Deposit ₦5,000 locks this rate till 20 Nov.”

## Service businesses
Sell a December package with a hard end date and a January retainer option.
Example: “3 content weeks in Nov + 1 January planning call.”
