# Ember daily ops (20 + 15 minutes)

## Morning (20 min)
- [ ] Check transfers / Paystack vs order list
- [ ] Pack yesterday’s paid orders first
- [ ] Message anyone whose item is delayed
- [ ] Post one Status: proof, stock, or deadline — not all three
- [ ] Confirm rider cash and POS float

## Night (15 min)
- [ ] Update inventory CSV qty
- [ ] Screenshot of today’s sales into a private album
- [ ] One follow-up to unpaid “I will pay later” chats
- [ ] Write tomorrow’s Status line now so you are not inventing it at 7am
- [ ] Stop work. Ember burnout creates refund chats.

## Do not
- Accept “pay on delivery” for custom or scarce SKUs
- Promise 48-hour delivery after 15 December without checking riders
- Give extra discount in the same chat after you already closed
