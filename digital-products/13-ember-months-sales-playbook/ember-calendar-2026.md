# Ember calendar 2026 (Nigeria SME)

Use one theme per week. Post daily on Status. Broadcast only to people who opted in.

## October — warm-up and Independence
- Week 1 (1–4 Oct): List dead stock. Price a “clearance before Ember” drop.
- Week 2 (5–11 Oct): Independence weekend. Patriotic bundle + gift-with-purchase, not 50% off everything.
- Week 3 (12–18 Oct): Collect deposits for November items with long supplier lead time.
- Week 4 (19–25 Oct): Soft-launch Black Friday waitlist. “Reply BF to get the list first.”
- Week 5 (26–31 Oct): Final restock orders. Freeze new SKUs unless they arrive before 20 Nov.

## November — Black Friday / Cyber Monday
- Week 1: Tease 3 SKUs only. Show cost of delay (“price goes up 1 Dec”).
- Week 2: Payment proof required to lock BF price. 40–50% deposit if made-to-order.
- BF week: One hero, one bundle, one last-chance. Stop inventing extra discounts mid-week.
- After BF: Honour prepaid orders first. Publicly post “delivery window” so chats stay calm.

## December — Christmas, travel, New Year
- 1–7 Dec: Gift bundles and corporate hampers. Close corporate invoices early.
- 8–14 Dec: Last date you can promise 20 Dec delivery. Write it on every quote.
- 15–21 Dec: Stop taking custom work you cannot finish. Sell ready stock.
- 22–26 Dec: Boxing / leftover drop. Honest “last sizes” Status.
- 27–31 Dec: New Year restock waitlist + January retainer for service businesses.

## Weekly 20-minute review (Sunday)
1. Cash in vs cash promised.
2. Orders paid vs orders packed.
3. Which Status got the most replies.
4. One SKU to kill next week.
5. One script that felt awkward — rewrite it.
