# Access and roles

Give the smallest access that still lets work happen. Ember volume is not a reason to share your bank app.

## Roles (pick one per person)

| Role | May do | Must not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat VA | Reply from scripts, log orders, send payment link | Change prices, issue refunds, share owner PIN |
| Shop assistant | Pack, confirm stock, take in-store cash | Approve last-price below floor, open bank app |
| Dispatch lead | Assign riders, update tracking, call failed drops | Pay riders from personal transfer without log |

## Phone and WhatsApp
- Business phone stays in the shop or with the named VA only.
- Two-step verification PIN is known by the owner only.
- Staff use their own labelled profile photo + first name in the chat greeting, not the owner’s name as if they were you.
- No forwarding of the business chat to a personal number.
- No installing extra WhatsApp clones on the business phone.

## Money
- Staff may send the pinned Paystack / transfer details only.
- Staff may confirm payment after the owner or the Sheets log shows it.
- Cash in a numbered pouch. Count at open and close. Two signatures if two people are on shift.

## Passwords
Owner keeps: bank, Paystack, hosting, domain, Apple/Google ID, 2FA codes.
Staff may have: Sheets editor on the order log only.
