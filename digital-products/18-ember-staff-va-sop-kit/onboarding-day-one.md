# Onboarding — first 90 minutes

Do this before the person is left alone with the phone.

## Minute 0–15 — identity and rules
1. Full name, phone, next of kin, start date on the shift log.
2. Read access-and-roles out loud. They initial the printed page.
3. Show the price floor list. Circle three items they must not discount.

## Minute 15–45 — tools
1. Open WhatsApp Business. Show labels: New, Quoted, Paid, Packed, Dispatched.
2. Show pinned messages: price list, Paystack link, return window.
3. Open the Sheets order log. One practice row together.
4. Show where stock photos live (one Drive folder, view-only).

## Minute 45–75 — three live chats
Owner sits beside them.
- Chat A: greeting + price.
- Chat B: “last price” — they use the floor script, no invention.
- Chat C: payment received — they log it, they do not celebrate until confirmed.

## Minute 75–90 — close drill
Walk the end-of-day checklist once even if it is morning. They must know how the day ends before they start.

## First-week rule
No solo night shift. No refunds. No supplier payments. Owner reviews the shift log every night for 7 days.
