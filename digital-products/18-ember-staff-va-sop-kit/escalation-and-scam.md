# Escalation and scam rules

Staff call or WhatsApp the owner immediately when any of these happen.

## Call now
- Customer asks to pay to a new account “the owner sent.”
- Screenshot of payment that does not match Paystack/bank alert.
- Request to share OTP, 2FA, or bank PIN “to confirm order.”
- Rider asks for extra cash outside the logged fee.
- Threat, police mention, or “I will expose you.”
- Refund demand above ₦[your cap, e.g. 15,000].

## Owner decides (log and wait)
- Last price below the printed floor.
- Custom order or bulk above ₦[cap].
- Return after the 7-day window.
- Staff wants to use personal transfer “just this once.”

## Never
- “Send me the code so I can release the item.”
- Installing AnyDesk / remote apps on the business phone.
- Paying a supplier from staff personal account for reimbursement later without a written owner yes.

## After a scare
1. Lock the phone.
2. Change WhatsApp two-step PIN (owner).
3. Review last 20 chats.
4. One Status if needed: “We only collect on the pinned account. Ignore other numbers.”
