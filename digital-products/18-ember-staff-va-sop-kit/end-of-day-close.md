# End-of-day close (15 minutes)

Do this before the phone leaves the counter.

1. Unread chats = 0, or labelled “Owner” if blocked.
2. Every paid order has a row in the Sheets log with amount and time.
3. Cash count vs opening float. Difference written, not explained later.
4. Packed vs pending list. Anything unpaid is not packed.
5. Phone lock. WhatsApp logged in. 2FA still owner-only.
6. Status: one honest caption for tomorrow, drafted, not posted if the owner posts Status.
7. Staff signs the shift log. Owner or next shift countersigns in the morning.

If cash is short: stop. Do not “borrow from tomorrow.” Call the owner before leaving.
