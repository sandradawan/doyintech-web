# WHT notes for invoices (Nigeria)

Not tax advice. Confirm current rates with your accountant (company vs individual, type of service).

## Invoice block you can paste

```
Fee (gross):                    ₦1,000,000
WHT (if Client deducts):        state rate on payment advice
Amount due if no WHT:           ₦1,000,000
If WHT applies: pay net + send credit note within 7 days.
```

## Good habits
- Invoice the **gross** fee, not the net you hope to receive.
- Put your TIN (if you have one) and bank details on every invoice.
- Ask in writing, before work starts: “Will you deduct WHT? At what rate?”
- File every credit note with the invoice PDF in one Drive folder: `Client / 2026-09 / INV-014`.
- If they “forget” the paper, send the chase script. Do not start the next phase.

## What WHT is (plain language)
Some companies must withhold a percentage of certain service payments and remit it to tax authorities. You should receive evidence so your accountant can use it. Without evidence, you may have given a discount you did not agree to.

## Red flags
- “We always deduct 10%, no paper.”
- Paying net months later with no credit note.
- Asking you to invoice a sister company after work is done.
