# WhatsApp chase scripts — WHT and late paper

Tone: calm, specific, dated. Send once, then follow the pause rule in your agreement.

## 1. Before they pay
Hi [Name] — invoice [INV-0XX] is ₦[gross]. If your accounts will deduct WHT, please confirm the rate today and send the credit note within 7 days of payment. I invoice the gross fee so the file stays clean for both accountants.

## 2. Paid net, no paper (day 3)
Hi [Name], thanks for the ₦[net] on [date]. Please send the WHT credit note / receipt for invoice [INV-0XX] this week so I can close the file. Happy to share my TIN again if accounts needs it.

## 3. Day 8 — still missing
Following up on invoice [INV-0XX]. Net landed on [date]; credit note is still outstanding. Per our agreement I will pause [next milestone] until the document is in my inbox or Drive.

## 4. They claim “we don’t issue those”
Understood if process is slow. I still need written confirmation of rate, amount withheld, and the period. A signed accounts email is a start; the official credit note should follow. I cannot treat the gap as a discount.

## 5. Scope creep while money is incomplete
I can look at the extra [page / feature] after [INV-0XX] is complete (net + WHT paper or full gross). Sending a one-line change order now so we do not mix the two.
