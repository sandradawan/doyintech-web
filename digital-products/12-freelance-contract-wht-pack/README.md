# Freelance Contract & WHT Survival Pack (Nigeria)

DoyinTech digital product — not legal advice. Have a lawyer review before you sign large deals.

## Who it is for
Nigerian freelancers and small studios who get paid by companies that deduct withholding tax (WHT), delay payment, or expand scope on WhatsApp.

## What is inside
- `service-agreement.md` — short service agreement you can paste into Docs
- `wht-invoice-notes.md` — how to show WHT on invoices without looking unprofessional
- `wht-chase-scripts.md` — WhatsApp lines for credit notes, receipts, and remittance proof
- `scope-change.md` — one-page change-order template
- `payment-milestones.csv` — Sheets-ready milestone tracker

## 20-minute setup
1. Copy the agreement. Fill your name, CAC/BN if any, bank, and scope one-liner.
2. Decide deposit (recommend 40–50% before work starts).
3. Add WHT language: “If you must deduct WHT, send the credit note / receipt within 7 days of payment.”
4. Import the CSV into Google Sheets.
5. Save the chase scripts as WhatsApp Quick Replies.
