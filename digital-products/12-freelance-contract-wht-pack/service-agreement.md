# Short Service Agreement (template)

**Not legal advice.** Adapt names, dates, and fees. Have counsel review for large retainers.

This Service Agreement is between:

- **Provider:** [Your name / business], [address], [email / WhatsApp]
- **Client:** [Company / person], [address], [email / WhatsApp]

**Start date:** [date]  
**End date / review date:** [date or “until deliverables accepted”]

## 1. Scope
Provider will deliver:

- [Deliverable 1]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

Out of scope unless a written change order is signed: extra pages, extra platforms, extra revision rounds, rush weekends, or new stakeholders mid-project.

## 2. Fees and payment
Total fee: ₦[amount] (or USD [amount] at the agreed naira rate on invoice date).

Schedule:
- Deposit: [40–50%] before work starts
- Mid: [optional]
- Final: before files / access / live deploy

Payment methods: bank transfer / Paystack. Work pauses if a milestone is more than 5 working days late.

## 3. Withholding tax (WHT)
If Client is required to deduct WHT under Nigerian tax rules:

1. Client states the rate on the payment advice.
2. Client pays the net amount and issues a WHT credit note / receipt within **7 days**.
3. Provider invoices the **gross** fee. Net received + documented WHT should equal the invoice.
4. Missing paperwork after 14 days is treated as an incomplete payment; Provider may pause work.

This clause does not give tax advice. Both parties confirm rates with their accountants.

## 4. Revisions
[2] revision rounds on the agreed draft. Further changes use the change-order form.

## 5. Timeline
Client provides assets and feedback within [2] working days of each request. Delays extend the calendar one-for-one.

## 6. Intellectual property
Upon **full payment**, Client receives a licence to use the final deliverables for the stated purpose. Provider may show non-confidential work in a portfolio unless Client objects in writing.

## 7. Confidentiality
Both parties keep non-public business information confidential for 12 months after the project ends.

## 8. Termination
Either party may end the project in writing. Client pays for work completed plus expenses. Deposit is non-refundable after work has started, unless Provider is in material breach.

## 9. Liability
Provider’s total liability is limited to fees paid in the 30 days before the claim, excluding fraud or personal injury.

## 10. Sign-off
WhatsApp “I agree” plus this document attached counts as acceptance if both parties are named above.
