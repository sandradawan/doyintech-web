# 2026 reminder board (Nigeria SME)

Use this as a wall / Notion / phone calendar. Confirm each date with your accountant.

## Recurring every month
| When | Item | Who typically owns it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7th | Pension remittance (employee + employer) | HR / founder |
| 10th | PAYE remittance to state LIRS (confirm your state) | Accountant |
| 14th | WHT schedules / receipts chase | Accountant |
| 21st | VAT return + remittance (if VAT-registered) | Accountant |
| Last Friday | File receipts, bank statements, invoice pack | Founder |

## Quarterly / annual (typical)
- **Q1 (Jan–Mar):** Prior-year books close, CIT self-assessment planning, WHT credits list.
- **Q2 (Apr–Jun):** CIT filing window planning, CAC annual returns prep, audited accounts if required.
- **Q3 (Jul–Sep):** Mid-year PAYE reconciliation, VAT health check, pension arrears sweep.
- **Q4 (Oct–Dec):** Budget 2027, stocktake, contractor WHT cleanup, December payroll cutoff.

## January 2026 focus
- Collect December invoices and bank statements.
- Confirm VAT registration status and filing portal login.
- Issue staff PAYE tax clearance request list if staff need it.

## June 2026 focus
- CAC annual returns pack: directors, shareholding, registered address.
- Confirm company secretary / status report if you use one.

## December 2026 focus
- Cut off sales invoices before year-end.
- List prepaid expenses and unpaid supplier bills.
- Book January accountant slot early.

## Penalty hygiene (operational)
- Never wait for a demand letter before reconciling VAT vs invoices.
- Keep PDF evidence: receipts, bank proofs, filed acknowledgements.
- If cash is tight, talk to your accountant *before* the due date — not after.
