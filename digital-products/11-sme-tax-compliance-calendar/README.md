# SME Tax & Compliance Calendar 2026 (Nigeria)

Operational reminder pack for Nigerian SMEs and solo operators who miss VAT, PAYE, WHT, pension, and CAC dates — then pay penalties.

**Not legal, tax, or accounting advice.** Deadlines and rates change. Confirm with your accountant and the current FIRS / LIRS / CAC notices before you file or pay.

## What you get
- `calendar-2026.md` — month-by-month reminder board
- `compliance-tracker.csv` — import to Google Sheets / Excel
- `whatsapp-reminder-scripts.md` — messages for your bookkeeper or yourself
- `accountant-handoff.md` — what to send your accountant every month

## 20-minute setup
1. Import `compliance-tracker.csv` into Google Sheets.
2. Fill Company name, TIN, VAT status, state (LIRS), pension PFA.
3. Put the 21st (VAT), 10th (PAYE — confirm your state), and 7th (pension) on your phone calendar.
4. Every Sunday, tick the tracker. Use the WhatsApp scripts if someone else files.
