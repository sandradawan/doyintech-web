# Monthly accountant handoff

Send this pack by the 5th of each month.

## Folder checklist
- [ ] Sales invoices (PDF or Sheets export)
- [ ] Purchase invoices / receipts
- [ ] Bank statements (all business accounts)
- [ ] POS / Paystack / Flutterwave settlement report
- [ ] Payroll register + new staff / exits
- [ ] Contractor invoices (for WHT)
- [ ] Prior month filing acknowledgements

## Message template
Subject / WhatsApp: [COMPANY] [MONTH] pack

1. Turnover this month (approx): ₦____
2. VAT-registered? Yes / No
3. New staff? Yes / No — names:
4. Big one-off expense? 
5. Questions for you:

## What you should get back
- Filed / not filed status for VAT, PAYE, pension, WHT
- Amounts due and account details
- Acknowledgement PDFs
- Open issues (missing TIN, unmatched receipts)
