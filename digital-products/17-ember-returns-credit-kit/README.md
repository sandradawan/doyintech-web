For Nigerian SMEs heading into Ember: more parcels means more “I no like am”, wrong sizes, and silent refund fights that eat October margin.

## What's inside

1. `return-policy.md` — 7-day window, who pays rider, what is not returnable.
2. `swap-scripts.md` — size/colour swap without a cash refund.
3. `credit-note.md` — store credit that keeps cash in the business.
4. `refund-scripts.md` — when you must refund, and how to close the chat.
5. `returns-log.csv` — order, reason, decision, rider cost, date closed.
6. `sunday-returns-checklist.md` — 20-minute weekly review before Ember volume spikes.

## How to start today (22 Sep 2026)

1. Post the 7-day policy on Status and pin it in WhatsApp Business.
2. Import `returns-log.csv`. One row per case — not one row per argument.
3. Default to **swap or credit note**. Cash refund is last, and only after the item is back.
4. Never refund before the parcel is in your hand unless the rider lost it.

Not legal advice. Align with your payment provider and state rules before Ember week.
