# WhatsApp Quote & Last-Price Kit

For Nigerian service SMEs and freelancers who lose deals on "last price?" chats.

## What you get
- Quote message template (copy into WhatsApp)
- Last-price / negotiation scripts that protect margin
- Deposit + Paystack / transfer wording
- Price-list CSV you can import to Sheets

## How to use in 15 minutes
1. Fill your rates in `price-list.csv`.
2. Send `quote-template.md` after a discovery chat.
3. When they ask last price, use scripts in `last-price-scripts.md`.
4. Lock the job with deposit lines in `deposit-and-paystack.md`.

Not legal advice. Adjust numbers to your market.
