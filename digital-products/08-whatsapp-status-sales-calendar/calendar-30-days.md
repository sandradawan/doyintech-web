# 30-day WhatsApp Status calendar

Post **2 Statuses most days**: morning (7:30–9:00) and evening (6:30–9:00). Keep each Status under 3 slides.

| Day | Theme | Morning | Evening | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Intro + proof | Who we are + 1 result | Price list / catalog | Trust |
| 2 | Product drop | Hero item + price | Behind-the-scenes prep | Enquiries |
| 3 | Social proof | Customer photo / quote | FAQ: delivery / location | Trust |
| 4 | Offer | Bundle or combo | Last-5 reminder | Orders |
| 5 | Education | How to choose / use | Mini tutorial | Saves |
| 6 | Restock | “Just in” photos | Size / flavour list | Enquiries |
| 7 | Review week 1 | Best seller this week | Soft CTA: chat to order | Pipeline |
| 8 | Authority | Process / hygiene / quality | Team or founder clip | Trust |
| 9 | Urgency | Limited stock | Countdown to weekend | Orders |
| 10 | Lifestyle | Customer using product | Before / after | Desire |
| 11 | Objection | “Is it expensive?” answer | Payment options (transfer / Paystack) | Close |
| 12 | UGC ask | Repost a buyer | “Send us your photo” | Content |
| 13 | Mid-month offer | Add-on or free delivery band | Terms in 1 slide | Orders |
| 14 | Review week 2 | Wins + thank you | Open slots / stock | Pipeline |
| 15 | New SKU | Launch 1 new item | Comparison vs old favourite | Enquiries |
| 16 | Location | Coverage areas + lag time | Pickup vs dispatch | Ops |
| 17 | Proof stack | 3 short testimonials | Screenshot of reviews | Trust |
| 18 | Flash | 12-hour deal | Sold / remaining | Orders |
| 19 | Education | Care / storage / warranty | Common mistakes | Saves |
| 20 | Soft close | “Chat ‘PRICE’ for list” | Voice-note Status if possible | Chats |
| 21 | Review week 3 | Top 3 products | Plan for week 4 | Pipeline |

| 23 | Community | Buyer shout-out | Referral: “tag a friend” | Reach |
| 24 | Objection | Delivery time honesty | Replacement policy | Close |
| 25 | Scarcity | Low stock list | Restock date | Orders |
| 26 | Premium | Best item + why it costs more | Payment plan if you offer one | AOV |
| 27 | Recap | Month highlights | Early-bird next drop | Desire |
| 28 | Review week 4 | Numbers you can share | Ask for reviews | Trust |
| 29 | Pre-sell | Next week drop teaser | Waitlist keyword (e.g. WAIT) | List |
| 30 | Reset | Full catalog refresh | Thank you + hours | Start again |

Repeat the cycle next month. Swap product names only — keep the rhythm.
