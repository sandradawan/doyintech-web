# WhatsApp Status Sales Calendar (30 Days)

**DoyinTech digital product** — for Nigerian / African SMEs who already get Status views but do not convert them into chats and orders.

## What you get
- `calendar-30-days.md` — day-by-day theme, best time slot, and posting goal
- `status-captions.md` — 90+ copy-paste captions (hooks, drops, proof, CTAs)
- `weekly-review-checklist.md` — 15-minute Friday review
- `status-tracker.csv` — log views, replies, and sales per Status

## How to use (15 minutes to start)
1. Pick your niche line in the captions file (fashion, food, services, digital).
2. Post **one Status in the morning slot** and **one in the evening slot** from the calendar.
3. Pin your catalog / price list as the first Status of the day when you restock.
4. Reply to every new chat within 30 minutes using the WhatsApp Growth Pack (sold separately) or your own greeting.
5. Log results in `status-tracker.csv` every Friday.
