# WhatsApp collection scripts (Nigeria SME)

Replace [Name], [Invoice], [Amount], [Due].
Keep receipts ready before you send.

## Day 0 — send invoice
Hi [Name], invoice *[Invoice]* for ₦[Amount] is ready. Due [Due].  
Pay to: [Bank] [Account] [Name]. I will send receipt once it lands.

## Day +2 — gentle
Hi [Name], checking that you saw *[Invoice]* (₦[Amount], due [Due]).  
Want me to resend the account details?

## Day +5 — clear ask
Hi [Name], *[Invoice]* is now due. Please complete ₦[Amount] today so we can keep your slot / next delivery.  
If you need to split, send a date and first instalment today.

## Day +10 — pause work
Hi [Name], we have not received ₦[Amount] on *[Invoice]*.  
We will pause new work / next batch until this clears. Reply with payment time today.

## Partial payment
Thanks, ₦[Paid] received. Balance ₦[Left] still open on *[Invoice]*. Next date?

## After payment
Confirmed ₦[Amount]. Receipt attached. Thank you — we continue.

## Rules
- One chase per message. No novels.
- Never argue price in the chase thread; take it to a call.
- Screenshot every promise of “I will pay tomorrow.”
