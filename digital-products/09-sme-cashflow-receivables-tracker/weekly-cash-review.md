# Weekly cash review (15 minutes)

Do this every Sunday night or Monday morning.

## 1. Balances
- [ ] Bank / POS / cash on hand written in `opening_balance` for the new week
- [ ] Last week’s `closing_balance` matches reality (±₦1,000)

## 2. In
- [ ] Log all collections in `receivables.csv` as Paid / Partial
- [ ] Move collected amounts into `cash_in_collections`

## 3. Out
- [ ] Mark supplier bills that must be paid this week (High)
- [ ] Do not pay Low priority if cash buffer is under 7 days of expenses

## 4. Risk
- [ ] List invoices > 7 days overdue
- [ ] Send one WhatsApp chase per overdue invoice (use scripts file)
- [ ] Pause new work for any client > 14 days overdue unless they pay a deposit

## 5. One decision
Write one sentence: “This week we will _____ so cash does not dip below ₦____.”
