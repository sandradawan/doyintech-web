# Slot calendar (no app required)

Use one Google Sheet tab or a paper diary. One row = one sellable slot.

## Suggested Ember capacity (example, tailor / makeup)

| Block | Weekday evenings | Sat | Sun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oct | 2 slots | 4 | 3 |
| Nov | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| 1–15 Dec | 3 | 6 | 4 |
| 16–24 Dec | 2 (finishing only) | 4 | 2 |
| 26–31 Dec | closed or premium only | 2 | 1 |

Rules that save December:

1. Never sell more Saturday slots than you can physically finish Friday night.
2. Keep one “repair / fitting / emergency” slot empty each Saturday until Wednesday.
3. Premium surcharge +20–40% after 15 Dec.
4. Travel jobs after 6pm need a driver budget in the quote.
5. If a deposit is more than 7 days late, release the slot and message the next person on the wait list.

Wait list columns: Name | Date wanted | Package | Phone | Added on | Status.
