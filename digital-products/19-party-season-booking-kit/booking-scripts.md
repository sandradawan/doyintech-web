# Booking scripts (copy into WhatsApp)

Replace [BRACKETS]. Send as separate bubbles, not one wall of text.

## 1. First reply (under 2 minutes)

Hi [NAME] — yes I still take [SERVICE] for Ember.

Quick check so I don’t double-book:
1. Event date + time
2. Location (area is enough)
3. Look / flavour / package you want

I’ll reply with the slot + deposit.

## 2. Slot + price

[DATE] [TIME] is open for [SERVICE].
Package: [NAME] — ₦[AMOUNT]
Deposit to hold: ₦[DEPOSIT] ( [PCT]% )
Balance: ₦[BALANCE] by [WHEN]

Reply PAY and I’ll send the account / Paystack link. Date is only reserved after deposit.

## 3. After they say “hold it” with no money

I can pencil [DATE] for 2 hours. After that it goes back to the open list.
Deposit ₦[DEPOSIT] locks it. Link: [PAYSTACK]

## 4. Deposit received

Deposit received — [DATE] [TIME] is yours.
I’ll send a 4-line brief form next. Please don’t change the date in the group chat; message me here.

## 5. Brief form (send as one message)

1. Full name + second contact
2. Exact address / landmark
3. Start time + how long you need me
4. Reference photos / menu / song list
5. Who pays the balance and by when

## 6. 72 hours before

Reminder: [DATE] [TIME] [AREA].
Balance ₦[BALANCE] due [DAY].
If the venue moves more than 30 minutes, transport is extra — tell me today.

## 7. Same-day “are you coming?”

On my way window is [TIME]. Please confirm the gate / estate code.
Balance before I start: ₦[BALANCE].

## 8. Soft no when the diary is full

That date is closed. Nearest open: [DATE] or [DATE].
If you want a junior / partner I trust, I can intro — deposit still applies.
