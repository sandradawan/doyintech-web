# Referral scripts (WhatsApp)

Ask only after a good delivery or a review. One ask per client per quarter.

## Soft intro ask

[NAME], if someone in your circle needs [SERVICE] before December, you can send them my number.
I treat referred clients as priority in the queue.

## Specific ask (better)

Who do you know that is restocking or launching a site this Ember season?
One name is enough. I will mention you sent them.

## Thank-you for a name

Received — I will text [THEIR NAME] today and keep you out of the negotiation.
Thank you.

## Small thank-you offer (optional)

If a referred job pays a 50% deposit, I will take ₦[AMOUNT] off your next invoice
OR send a ₦[AMOUNT] airtime/data thank-you — your choice.
Keep the thank-you under 5% of the referred job so margin stays intact.

## When they say “I don’t know anyone”

No problem. If it comes up later, my Status has the offer pinned.
