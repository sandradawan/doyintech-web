# Sunday harvest — 20 minutes

1. Export last week’s delivered jobs into `harvest-tracker.csv`.
2. Send Day-1 review script to anyone delivered 1–14 days ago with no review.
3. Send Day-3 nudge to anyone asked 3+ days ago with no reply.
4. Send one referral ask to clients who already reviewed.
5. Pick one approved testimonial for Monday Status.
6. Mark permission_to_post before publishing a name or photo.
7. Stop at 20 minutes. Volume beats perfect wording.
