# Deposit rules (keep 15%+ after fees)

Use this before you advertise a "small deposit."

## Formula

- Selling price S (what the customer pays).
- True cost C (supplier + wrapping + dispatch + 1.5% transfer/Paystack cushion).
- Floor margin M = 0.15 × S (do not go below).
- Minimum deposit D = max(0.30 × S, C × 0.6).

If D would leave you unable to restock that SKU, raise D or drop the SKU.

## Worked example

- Ankara set S = ₦25,000
- Cost C = ₦16,000
- Floor margin = ₦3,750
- Deposit D = max(₦7,500, ₦9,600) = ₦9,600 → round to ₦10,000
- Balance = ₦15,000 on pickup

## Rules to paste in your catalog description

1. Deposit locks colour/size for this batch only.
2. Deposit is transferable to another SKU of equal or higher price in the same batch.
3. Deposit is not refundable after production starts (state the cut-off date).
4. Balance due before handover. No "I will send when I reach."
5. If you miss the window by 7 days with no SHIFT message, the item goes to the waitlist. Deposit becomes store credit for 30 days.

## What not to do

- Do not accept "my guy will pay." One name, one number, one receipt.
- Do not start production on a promise screenshot.
- Do not discount the balance to "close" — that eats M.
