# Prepay math (protect the float)

Use this before you transfer.

## Rule of thumb
- New supplier: never more than 30% until first clean arrival.
- Known supplier, 3+ good drops: 40–50% to lock price.
- Full prepay: only if goods are already packed and you have waybill + photos today.

If the 50% prepay exceeds your safe cap, cut quantity. Do not borrow float from rent.

## Split SKUs
Lock 2 fast movers at 50% rather than 6 slow SKUs at 100%. Ember rewards availability of proven lines.

## FX / agent buys
Write the naira amount you will actually send today, not last month’s rate. Recalc if the agent quotes dollars.
