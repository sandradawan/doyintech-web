# Arrival calendar — Ember 2026

Tick one “in” week per SKU. Do not stack every arrival in the same week as Independence + Black Friday.

| Week starting | Theme | What should arrive | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 29 Sep 2026 | Lock POs | Fabrics, packaging, core SKUs | Full prepay to new vendors |
| 6 Oct | Independence leftover | Fast fashion / party wear inputs | New untested SKUs |
| 13 Oct | Mid-Oct buffer | Beauty, gadgets refill | Oversize imports without duty cash |
| 20 Oct | Corporate hamper inputs | Bottles, wraps, snacks | Late wine without storage |
| 27 Oct | Halloween / party | Decor, makeup | Anything with 3-week sea freight |
| 3 Nov | Black Friday prep | Hero SKUs + extra packaging | Experimental lines |
| 10 Nov | Black Friday week | Nothing critical (ops week) | New supplier first drop |
| 17 Nov | Recovery restock | Top 5 SKUs only | Deep discounts on dead stock |
| 24 Nov | December lock | Gift SKUs | Open-ended “we go send” |
| 1 Dec | Last safe inbound | Local market only | Ports / long intercity |
| 8 Dec | Sell-down | No inbound unless prepaid retail | New POs |
| 15 Dec | Close new POs | Zero inbound risk | Deposits for January goods from Ember cash |
