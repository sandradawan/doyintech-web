# Supplier WhatsApp scripts

Replace [NAME], [SKU], [QTY], [NGN], [WEEK].

## 1. First touch (existing supplier)

Good morning [NAME]. Ember restock. I want [SKU] × [QTY] for delivery week of [WEEK].

Please confirm:
1. Unit price today
2. Minimum order
3. Deposit % to lock
4. Ready date (not “soon”)
5. Who pays last-mile to my shop / warehouse

I pay deposit only after you send a written ready date.

## 2. First touch (new supplier)

Hi [NAME], I retail [CATEGORY] in [CITY]. Need a quote for [SKU] × [QTY], Ember window.

Same 5 questions as above. I work prepaid after a sample or a small first order.

## 3. Push back on 100% upfront

I can do 40–50% now to lock the batch, balance on photo of packed goods + waybill number.

Full prepay only if you have done 3 clean deliveries with me this year.

## 4. Chase (3 days late)

[NAME], goods for PO-[ID] were due [DATE]. Please send:
- new ready date
- photo of stock
- rider / waybill

If it slips past [CUTOFF] I will reallocate the deposit to another SKU. Confirm today.

## 5. Short shipment

Received [QTY_GOT] of [QTY_PAID]. Please either:
A) send the balance this week, or
B) credit [NGN] against next PO.

Reply with A or B.

## 6. Quality fail

These units fail our check: [PHOTO]. Options: replace this week, or credit. I will not list damaged stock for Ember.
