# Move Ember buyers off cash-on-delivery

POD is the #1 reason Ember parcels bounce. Use this sequence.

## Status line (post 3x / week in October)

Ember dispatch is prepaid. You pick a window, you pay, the rider leaves. No gate arguments. Link in bio / chat me SKU.

## First reply when they say "pay on delivery"

I can hold [SKU] until [time today]. Ember riders only leave when the transfer / Paystack receipt is in. I send tracking the minute it moves. Account: [or Paystack link].

## Soft objection: "What if it is fake?"

You get a sealed photo before the rider leaves + a 24-hour defect chat. That is safer than cash at a gate when networks are down.

## Repeat buyer exception

If they have 3 prepaid orders with you this year, you may allow 50% now / 50% before the rider leaves — still not full POD.

## Math reminder

A ₦25,000 dress + ₦3,500 rider + one failed POD = you ate the rider fee and lost the slot. Two of those a week wipes an Ember weekend.
