# Ember dispatch rules (copy into your Status + price list)

## Default (1 Oct – 20 Dec)

- Orders paid before **2:00pm WAT** leave same day if stock is on the shelf.
- Orders paid after 2:00pm leave the next working day.
- **No new Lagos Island drops after 3:30pm** (traffic + estate gates).
- Sundays: dispatch only if the customer already paid AND confirmed a receiver on site.

## Payment rule

| Order size | Ember rule |
| --- | --- |
| Under ₦8,000 | Prepaid preferred. POD only for repeat buyers you know. |
| ₦8,000–₦15,000 | 50% before dispatch, balance before rider leaves your street. |
| Above ₦15,000 | 100% prepaid. No exceptions in Ember. |

## City windows (edit to match your rider)

- **Lagos Mainland:** 10:00–16:00. Estates: send gate code in the brief.
- **Lagos Island / VI / Ikoyi:** 10:00–15:00. Add ₦1,500–2,500 island surcharge if the rider charges it.
- **Abuja:** 10:00–16:30. Confirm estate / church / office security list.
- **Port Harcourt:** 10:00–15:30. Rain days: next-day only.
- **Intercity (GIG / Kwik / bus park):** hand over before 12:00. Customer pays last-mile from the park unless you quoted door-to-door.

## Failed-delivery cap

Two attempts included. Third attempt is a paid redispatch (quote the exact fee). After 7 days of no confirmed window, treat as return-to-seller and offer store credit minus the two rider fees.
