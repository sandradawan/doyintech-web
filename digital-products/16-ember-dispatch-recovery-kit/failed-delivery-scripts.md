# Failed-delivery scripts

Replace [brackets]. Keep one thread per order.

## 1. Rider: customer not picking

Hi [Name], rider is at [landmark] with [SKU]. I called twice. Reply **NOW** with:  
A) Coming out  
B) Leave with [person + phone]  
C) New window today  
If I do not hear in 10 minutes the rider leaves and the next drop is a paid retry.

## 2. Customer: "I am not around"

No problem. Ember slots fill fast so I will not keep the rider waiting. Send a window that someone is on site. Retry fee is [₦X] unless you pick up yourself by [day].

## 3. Wrong address / estate refused entry

The estate asked for a code / name on the list. Send both now. If we cannot enter in 15 minutes this counts as attempt 1 of 2.

## 4. Customer wants to inspect and pay less

The price on the receipt stands. Rider can wait 3 minutes for you to check seal and qty. We do not renegotiate at the gate. If you decline, parcel returns and deposit covers the two rider legs.

## 5. Rider went silent

Sent to rider: Please drop live location + photo of the street now. If I do not see it in 20 minutes I reassign and your fee for this run is cancelled.

To customer: Small delay — I am moving your parcel to a second rider. New ETA [time]. Sorry for the wait.

## 6. After two fails

We tried [date] and [date]. I have paused dispatch. Reply with a firm window this week or I convert the order to store credit minus ₦[fees]. I will not send a third time on guesswork.
