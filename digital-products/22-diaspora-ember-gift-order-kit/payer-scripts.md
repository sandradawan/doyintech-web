# Diaspora payer vs local receiver scripts

Replace [SHOP], [ITEM], [NGN], [USD], [DATE], [ADDRESS].

## First reply (local person shopping)
Thanks for shopping [SHOP]. Who is paying — you, or family abroad?  
If abroad: send their WhatsApp + the gift list. We confirm price in Naira, then they pay. We deliver in Nigeria and send photo + video proof.

## First reply (payer abroad)
Hi — [SHOP] in Nigeria. We can buy and deliver for your family.  
Send: 1) items or budget 2) receiver name + phone + area 3) latest date.  
We reply with a Naira quote + USD/GBP equivalent at today’s rate + 5% buffer.

## Quote lock
Quote valid 24 hours (FX moves). Pay Paystack/transfer. We do not start until funds hit.

## “My sister will pay later”
We hold the list 24h. After that the price can change. Share this message with the payer.

## Proof of delivery
Delivered to [NAME] at [AREA] on [DATE]. Photo + short video attached. Keep this chat as receipt.
