# DoyinTech — US & UK Outreach System

**Important:** Outreach is done by *you* (email, LinkedIn, form, WhatsApp). This pack gives targets, scripts, and a daily system. Do not spam; personalize every message.

## Positioning

You are **not** selling “a website to people with no site only.”  
You are selling **digital fixes that make local businesses look trustworthy and get more enquiries**:

- Dated / weak website redesign  
- Mobile fix + speed  
- Online booking / enquiry forms  
- WhatsApp/automation workflows  
- SEO + Google Business polish  
- Custom tools (invoices, portals, booking)

**Lead magnet:** Free **3-minute digital audit** (2 specific problems + 1 recommended fix).

## Ideal prospects

Beauty salons · Makeup artists · Property / estate agents · Restaurants · Cleaning companies · Construction trades · Consultants · Coaches · Local shops · Accountants / professional services

### Qualification (must pass 2+)

- [ ] No website, or site looks 5+ years old  
- [ ] Site broken, slow, or not mobile-friendly  
- [ ] No online booking / weak enquiry path  
- [ ] Thin or missing Google Business presence  
- [ ] Heavy reliance on phone/WhatsApp only  
- [ ] Active business (recent reviews, open hours, social posts)

**Skip:** Scam signals, closed businesses, enterprise corporations, anyone who already has a strong modern site + agency.
