## Using the templates in Canva

1. In Canva, click **Create a design › Import file** (or drag the .pptx file onto the Canva home page).
2. Canva turns every slide into an editable page.
3. Click any text to change it. Drag your own photo onto a "Your photo" placeholder to replace it.
4. Change colours: click a shape, then the colour box in the top bar. To change every use of a colour at once, select the colour and choose **Change all**.
5. Download each page as **PNG** (posts) or **PNG/MP4** (stories) and post it.

Canva may swap the fonts for similar ones on import. Pick your brand font in Canva if you want a different look.

## Using the templates in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Keynote

- **PowerPoint:** open the file, edit the text, and right-click a photo › **Change Picture** to use your own. Then **File › Export › PNG** (choose "All slides" or "Just this one").
- **Google Slides:** go to slides.google.com › **File › Open › Upload**. Right-click a photo › **Replace image**. Download each slide with **File › Download › PNG image**.
- **Keynote:** open the .pptx file directly, then **File › Export To › Images**.

## Make them yours in 10 minutes

1. Pick the colour style closest to your brand, or change the accent colour to yours.
2. Replace **@yourbrand** and the small circle (your logo) on each design.
3. Duplicate the templates you'll use most (tips, reviews, offers) so you always have a fresh copy.
4. Use real numbers, reviews and prices. Never post a statistic you can't back up.

## Posting tips

- **Stories:** keep important text away from the top and bottom 250 pixels; the app's buttons cover those areas.
- **Carousels:** use template 4 as the cover, template 5 for each point (duplicate it), and template 6 to end with a clear call to action.
- **Consistency beats perfection:** two to four posts a week, every week, works better than daily posts for one week.
