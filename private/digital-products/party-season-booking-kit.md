# Party Season Booking & Deposit Kit

DoyinTech digital product — instant download after Paystack.
Full working files live in the repo folder `digital-products/19-party-season-booking-kit/`.

## You get

- WhatsApp scripts: first reply, slot + deposit, confirmation, 72-hour reminder
- Pin-able deposit and no-show policy
- Ember slot-capacity table (Oct–Dec)
- Google Sheets bookings CSV
- Status captions that sell remaining dates
- Sunday 20-minute capacity review

## Rule that pays for this kit

Do not confirm a date until the deposit hits. Pencil for 2 hours max.

## Quick start

1. Pin the short policy.
2. Import `csv/bookings.csv`.
3. Save first-reply as a Quick Reply.
4. Post one open-slot Status today.
