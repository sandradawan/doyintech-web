## 3. Chores by age
**Ages 4–5:** put toys away, put dirty clothes in the basket, feed a pet with help, set spoons on the table, water plants.
**Ages 6–7:** make the bed, sort laundry by colour, wipe the table, sweep a small area, pack their school bag.
**Ages 8–9:** wash plates, fold clothes, sweep and mop a room, help with simple cooking, tidy their wardrobe.
**Ages 10–12:** cook a simple meal with supervision, iron their uniform, wash their own socks and underwear, clean the bathroom sink, help younger siblings with reading.

## 5. Star reward chart
Each good choice earns a star. Agree the rewards together **before** you start, and keep promises: a reward that never comes stops the chart working.

| Stars | Reward (agree together) |
|---|---|
| 10 | Choose the Friday-night film or game |
| 25 | A trip to the park, beach or a friend's house |
| 50 | A small treat or toy (set a budget, e.g. NGN 3,000) |
| 100 | A special day out |

**What earns a star:** finishing the morning routine, homework without reminders, kindness to a sibling, trying a new food, owning up to a mistake, reading an extra chapter.
**What does not cost stars:** never take stars away. Missing a star is consequence enough, and charts that punish quickly get ignored.

## 8. Four-week holiday plan
A loose plan beats endless "I'm bored". Mix one learning activity, one active activity and one creative activity every day.

**Week 1: Settle in**
- Mon: Make a holiday wish list together. Start the reading log.
- Tue: Bake or cook something simple (puff-puff, pancakes, fruit salad).
- Wed: Nature hunt: find 10 different leaves and stick them in a book.
- Thu: Daily maths challenge at doyintech.com/kids, then a board game.
- Fri: Build a fort or den from chairs and bedsheets. Film night.
- Weekend: Visit grandparents or family. Write them a thank-you card.

**Week 2: Make things**
- Mon: Draw a comic strip with 6 boxes about a superhero from your street.
- Tue: Make a musical instrument from bottles, beans and rubber bands.
- Wed: Plant seeds (beans, tomatoes or pepper) in cups and start a growth diary.
- Thu: Write and post a letter to a friend or cousin.
- Fri: Talent show for the family. Everyone does one act.
- Weekend: Help plan and shop for one family meal with a budget.

**Week 3: Explore**
- Mon: Learn 10 words in a new language (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French).
- Tue: Map your neighbourhood: draw roads, shops, church/mosque, school.
- Wed: Science day: make a volcano with baking soda and vinegar.
- Thu: Interview a grandparent about their childhood and write it up.
- Fri: Treasure hunt with clues around the house.
- Weekend: Visit a museum, zoo, library, park or beach.

**Week 4: Get ready for school**
- Mon: Sort out school bag, books and uniform. Make a list of what is needed.
- Tue: Practise times tables with the 60-second game at doyintech.com/kids.
- Wed: Write a "My Holiday" story with a beginning, middle and end.
- Thu: Reset bedtimes and wake-up times to school-day times.
- Fri: Celebration: count up reading-log books and stars, give the rewards.
