## 5. Petty cash voucher

**PETTY CASH VOUCHER No. [ ]** Date: ______
Paid to: ______________________
Amount: ₦ ______ (in words: ______________________ naira only)
Purpose: ________________________________
Receipt attached: ☐ Yes ☐ No (explain): ______
Received by ______ Approved by ______ Paid by (custodian) ______

**Imprest rules**
- Fixed float of ₦[50,000]; replenish weekly against receipts.
- No single payment above ₦[10,000] from petty cash.
- Custodian counts cash daily; cash + vouchers must always equal the float.
- No IOUs. No personal loans from petty cash.

## 11. Office opening and closing checklist
**Opening:** alarm off · lights and AC on · check generator fuel and inverter · reception tidy · phones and WhatsApp line on · check emails/voicemail.
**Closing:** windows and back doors locked · ACs and sockets off · cash locked in safe · laptops stored · generator off · alarm on · keys with [ ].

## 12. Official letter layout (Nigerian format)
Letterhead → reference number and date (right) → recipient's title and address (left) → salutation → bold underlined subject → body → "Yours faithfully" (Dear Sir/Madam) or "Yours sincerely" (named person) → signature, name and title → "cc:" list if any.
