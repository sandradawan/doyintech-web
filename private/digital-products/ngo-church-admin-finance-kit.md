# NGO & Church Admin and Finance Kit
**DoyinTech · for churches, mosques, NGOs, associations, cooperatives and alumni groups**

Simple, trustworthy systems for money and records, so members and donors can see where every naira went. Includes templates, policies and two spreadsheets.

---

## 1. The five money rules that prevent most problems
1. **Two signatories** on every bank account; no single person can move money.
2. **All income goes to the bank first.** Count cash offerings/dues with two people present, record the amount on a count sheet both sign, and deposit within [48 hours].
3. **No payment without a voucher** approved by someone other than the person receiving the money.
4. **Monthly report** to the executive or board, every month, even when nothing changed.
5. **Annual accounts** presented at the AGM, ideally reviewed by an independent accountant.

## 5. Annual budget template
See **org-budget.csv**. Columns: Category · Line item · Budget (₦) · Actual Q1–Q4 · Total actual · Variance.

Typical lines:
- **Income:** tithes/offerings, dues, donations, grants, event income, project levies, interest.
- **Expenditure:** rent/maintenance, salaries/honoraria, utilities and fuel, welfare, outreach/programmes, events, transport, bank charges, audit/legal, savings/reserve.

Rule of thumb: keep a reserve of at least [3] months of fixed costs.

## 7. Project/donor grant tracking
For each grant or special project, keep a separate sheet or ledger:
- Donor name, amount pledged, amount received, dates.
- What it must be spent on (restricted vs unrestricted).
- Every expense with receipt and photo evidence.
- Beneficiary list (with consent) and outputs (e.g. 120 students supported).
- Report deadlines.

Never spend restricted money on general running costs, even temporarily.

## 9. Membership register
Name · Phone · Email · Address · Date joined · Unit/department · Dues status · Birthday · Next of kin. Keep it in a spreadsheet with access limited to two officers. Ask members' consent before sharing their details in group chats.

---

## 10. Annual report outline (for AGM and donors)
1. Message from the chair/pastor/director
2. Who we are and what we did this year (3–5 highlights with numbers and photos)
3. Programmes and outreach — people reached, outcomes
4. Financial summary — income by source, expenditure by category (two pie charts), closing balance
5. Thank-you to donors and partners
6. Plans for next year and how people can support

---

## 11. Policies to adopt (one paragraph each)
- **Conflict of interest:** officers declare any personal interest in a payment or contract and step out of the decision.
- **Procurement:** three quotes for purchases above ₦[ ]; the person who requests cannot approve.
- **Safeguarding:** two adults present with children; report concerns to [designated person].
- **Data:** member data used only for the organisation's purposes, never sold or shared.
- **Social media:** only designated officers post on official pages.

---

## 12. Year-end checklist
- [ ] Bank statements reconciled for all 12 months
- [ ] All vouchers filed with receipts
- [ ] Fixed assets list updated (instruments, chairs, vehicles, generators)
- [ ] Accounts prepared and reviewed
- [ ] Annual report drafted
- [ ] Returns filed where registered (CAC Part F / incorporated trustees annual returns)
- [ ] Signatories updated after elections
