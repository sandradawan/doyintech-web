## Part 1 — How funders read your plan

Reviewers for SME loans, grant programmes and angel investors usually spend less than ten minutes on a first read. They look for four things:

1. **A real customer** who already pays for a solution today.
2. **Numbers that add up**: price × customers − costs.
3. **Evidence**: sales so far, pre-orders, letters of intent, photos of the shop.
4. **A clear use of funds**: exactly what the money buys and how it increases sales.

Write plainly. Avoid "We are the leading…" unless you can prove it. Specific beats impressive: *"42 paying customers in Ikeja since March"* beats *"huge market potential"*.

### 1. Executive summary (half a page — write it last)
- What you sell and to whom.
- Proof so far (revenue, customers, growth).
- How much you need and what it buys.
- What the money will achieve in 12 months (e.g. monthly revenue from ₦1.2m to ₦3m).

### 2. The business
Legal form (business name / limited company), date started, location, opening hours, number of staff.

### 3. Problem and solution
- The problem, in the customer's words.
- What they do today and why it isn't good enough (price, distance, quality, trust).
- Your product or service and how it is better.

### 4. Market
- **Target customer:** who exactly (e.g. working mothers in Lekki and Ajah, aged 28–45).
- **Market size:** count from the bottom up. *"There are about 300 schools within 10 km; 20% buy uniforms from outside suppliers; average order ₦1.5m a year."*
- **Trends:** why now (price changes, regulation, behaviour moving online).

## Part 3 — 10-slide pitch deck outline

1. **Title** — name, one-line description, your name and contact.
2. **Problem** — one sentence and one photo or statistic.
3. **Solution** — what you sell; a product photo or screenshot.
4. **Traction** — revenue chart, customer count, partnerships. (Most important slide.)
5. **Market** — bottom-up size for the next 2–3 years.
6. **Business model** — how you make money, price and margin.
7. **Competition** — a simple table or 2×2 chart; why you win.
8. **Go-to-market** — channels and cost to acquire a customer.
9. **Team** — 2–4 people, relevant experience only.
10. **The ask** — amount, use of funds (pie chart), milestones it unlocks.

Rules: one idea per slide, large numbers, no paragraphs, 10 slides, deliverable as PDF under 5 MB.
