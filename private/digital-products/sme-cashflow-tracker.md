# SME Cashflow & Receivables Tracker
DoyinTech · After purchase

## Setup (Google Sheets or Excel)
Columns for Cash in: Date | Source | Amount | Notes
Columns for Cash out: Date | Vendor | Amount | Category
Receivables: Client | Invoice # | Amount | Due | Status | Last chase
Payables: Vendor | Amount | Due | Status

## Sunday 15-minute review
1. Total cash in this week
2. Total cash out
3. Who still owes you (chase list)
4. Bills due next 7 days

## WhatsApp chase (polite)
Hi [Name], gentle reminder: invoice [id] for ₦[amount] was due [date].
Please confirm payment today or reply with a date. Thank you.
