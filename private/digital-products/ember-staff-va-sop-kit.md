# Ember Staff & VA WhatsApp SOP Kit

Hire extra Ember hands without handing them your bank app.

## Roles
Chat VA replies from scripts and logs orders. Shop assistant packs and takes in-store cash. Dispatch lead assigns riders. Nobody changes prices below the floor or holds 2FA.

## Day-one
90 minutes: identity, price floor, labels (New / Quoted / Paid / Packed / Dispatched), three live chats beside the owner, then a close drill.

## Staff greeting
Hi [Name], this is [Staff] at [Shop]. I can help with stock, price, and delivery.

## Last price
I can do ₦[floor] if you pay now. Below that I ask the owner.

## Close
Unread = 0 or labelled Owner. Cash counted. Phone locked. Shift log signed.

## Call the owner
New account “the owner sent,” OTP requests, payment screenshots with no alert, refunds above your cap.

Import `csv/staff-shift-log.csv`. One row per shift.
