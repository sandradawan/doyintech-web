# HR & Staff Policy Starter Pack
**DoyinTech · for Nigerian SMEs with 3–50 staff**

Everything a small company needs to hire, manage and part ways with staff properly: an employee handbook you can adapt, letters HR sends every month, a leave policy, an appraisal form and two tracking sheets.

> How to use: copy each template into Word or Google Docs, replace everything in [BRACKETS], put it on your letterhead, and have a lawyer review the handbook once before you roll it out. This pack is a practical starting point, not legal advice.

---

## Part 1 — Employee handbook template

### 1. Welcome
Welcome to [COMPANY NAME]. This handbook explains how we work, what we expect from you and what you can expect from us. It forms part of your terms of employment together with your offer letter. Where the two differ, your offer letter applies.

### 2. Working hours and attendance
- Normal hours: [8:00 am – 5:00 pm], Monday to Friday, with a [one-hour] lunch break.
- Resumption after [8:15 am] is recorded as late. Three lates in one month attract a written warning.
- If you will be absent, call or WhatsApp your line manager before [9:00 am]. A message to a colleague is not notice.
- Absence for more than [three] consecutive working days without notice may be treated as abandonment of duty.

### 3. Probation
New staff serve a probation of [three/six] months. During probation either party may end employment with [one week's] notice or pay in lieu. At the end of probation your manager will confirm you in writing, extend probation once, or end employment.

### 4. Pay and deductions
- Salaries are paid by bank transfer on or before the [last working day] of each month.
- Statutory deductions: PAYE income tax, pension (8% employee contribution) and, where applicable, NHF (2.5% of basic).
- The company contributes 10% to your pension and remits deductions to the relevant authorities.
- Salary advances are at management's discretion, capped at [50%] of one month's net pay and recovered over at most [three] months.

### 5. Leave
See the Leave Policy in Part 3.

### 6. Code of conduct
You must:
- treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect;
- protect company money, equipment, customer data and passwords;
- declare any conflict of interest, including side businesses that sell to our customers;
- dress [smart casual / in company uniform] during work hours.

The following are gross misconduct and may lead to summary dismissal after a fair hearing: theft or fraud, collecting customer payments into a personal account, violence or threats, sexual harassment, working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, deliberate damage to company property, and sharing confidential information.

### 7. Phones, WhatsApp and social media
- Company WhatsApp lines and social accounts belong to the company. Hand over passwords and two-step PINs when you leave.
- Never post customer details, prices agreed privately or internal disputes online.
- Personal phone use during work hours should be brief.

### 8. Discipline procedure
1. Verbal warning (recorded on file).
2. Written query — you will be given [48 hours] to respond in writing.
3. Written warning or suspension (with or without pay for up to [two weeks]).
4. Final warning.
5. Termination.

Serious cases may skip steps. You always have the right to respond in writing and to be heard before a final decision.

### 9. Grievances
Raise any complaint with your line manager first. If unresolved within [five] working days, write to [the MD / HR]. Complaints about your manager may go directly to [the MD].

### 10. Resignation and exit
- Notice: [one month] after confirmation, or pay in lieu.
- Return all company property, documents and passwords on or before your last day.
- Final pay, less any outstanding advances, is paid on the next payroll date after clearance.

### Acknowledgement
I have read and understood this handbook.
Name: ____________ Signature: ____________ Date: ____________

## Part 2 — Letters HR sends

### 2.1 Offer of employment
[DATE]

Dear [NAME],

**OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT — [JOB TITLE]**

We are pleased to offer you the position of [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY NAME], reporting to [MANAGER TITLE], with effect from [START DATE].

1. **Remuneration:** a gross monthly salary of ₦[AMOUNT], made up of basic ₦[ ], housing ₦[ ] and transport ₦[ ], subject to statutory deductions.
2. **Probation:** [three] months, after which your appointment may be confirmed in writing.
3. **Hours:** [8:00 am – 5:00 pm], Monday to Friday.
4. **Leave:** [15] working days of annual leave after confirmation.
5. **Notice:** [one week] during probation, [one month] after confirmation.

Please sign and return a copy of this letter by [DATE] to confirm your acceptance.

Yours faithfully,
[NAME], [TITLE]

Accepted: ____________ Date: ____________

### 2.2 Query letter
[DATE]
To: [NAME], [JOB TITLE]

**QUERY: [SHORT DESCRIPTION, e.g. LATENESS TO WORK]**

It has been observed that on [DATE(S)] you [describe facts only, e.g. resumed at 9:40 am without prior notice]. This is contrary to section [ ] of the employee handbook.

You are required to explain in writing, within [48 hours] of receiving this letter, why disciplinary action should not be taken against you.

[NAME], [TITLE]

### 2.3 Warning letter
**WRITTEN WARNING**
Following your response dated [DATE] to the query of [DATE], management is not satisfied with your explanation. This letter serves as a [first / final] written warning. A repeat of this conduct within [six] months may lead to further disciplinary action, including termination. A copy of this letter will be kept on your file.

### 2.4 Confirmation of appointment
We are pleased to confirm your appointment as [JOB TITLE] with effect from [DATE], following satisfactory completion of your probation. All other terms of your offer letter remain unchanged.

### 2.5 Termination letter
Further to [the disciplinary hearing held on DATE / the restructuring announced on DATE], management has decided to terminate your employment with effect from [DATE]. You will be paid [salary up to DATE, accrued leave allowance and one month's pay in lieu of notice], less any outstanding liabilities, upon clearance. Please return all company property to [NAME] by [DATE].

### 2.6 Reference letter
To whom it may concern: [NAME] was employed by [COMPANY] as [JOB TITLE] from [DATE] to [DATE]. Their duties included [ ]. During this period they were [punctual / reliable / honest]. We would [recommend them without reservation]. Contact: [PHONE / EMAIL].

## Part 6 — The two sheets in this pack
