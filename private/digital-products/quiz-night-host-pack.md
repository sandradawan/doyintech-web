# Quiz Night Host Pack
**DoyinTech · 230 questions in 8 rounds, with answers, scorecard and host script**

Everything you need to run a fun quiz for a church social, school event, office party, birthday or family gathering. Questions run from easy (*) to hard (***), so children and adults can play together.

---

## How to run your quiz night (host script)
**Before the night**
- Choose 4–6 rounds (about 10 minutes each). A two-hour event fits 6 rounds with a break.
- Print one answer sheet per team per round and one scorecard for the scorer.
- Teams of 3–6 work best. Mix ages so everyone can help.
- Prepare prizes: one for the winners, one for the best team name, one for last place.

**Opening (read aloud)**
"Good evening everyone, and welcome to quiz night! Tonight there are [NUMBER] rounds of questions. Write your team name on every answer sheet. No phones, no searching online: honesty makes it fun! I'll read each question twice. At the end of each round, swap sheets with the team next to you for marking. Let's begin with Round 1: [ROUND NAME]."

**During each round**
- Read each question twice, slowly, with its number.
- Give 30 seconds per question; announce "last 10 seconds".
- After the round, read the answers and the fun facts. People love the facts.
- Collect sheets and update the scorecard. Announce the top three teams after each round.

**Tie-breaker**
Ask one number question: "In metres, how high is Mount Kilimanjaro?" (Answer: about 5,895.) The closest team wins.

**Closing**
"Thank you all for playing! Congratulations to [WINNING TEAM]. A big round of applause for everyone, and thank you to [HOST/ORGANISERS]."
