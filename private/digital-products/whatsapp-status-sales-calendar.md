# WhatsApp Status Sales Calendar (30 Days)
DoyinTech · After purchase

## How to use
Post 1 Status/day. Mix proof, offer, tip. Always end with a reply word (PRICE / BOOK / YES).

## Week 1
Day 1: Offer + price anchor · Reply PRICE
Day 2: Before/after or result · Reply BOOK
Day 3: Tip related to your service
Day 4: Limited slots this week
Day 5: Customer quote
Day 6: FAQ (location, time, payment)
Day 7: Soft recap + weekend booking

## Week 2–4
Rotate: restock, bundle save, urgency (today only), social proof, behind-the-scenes, clear CTA.

## Weekly review (Sunday)
- How many chats started?
- Which Status got replies?
- Repeat winners next week.
