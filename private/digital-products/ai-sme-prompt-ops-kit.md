# AI SME Prompt & Ops Kit — DoyinTech

## How to use
1. Pick one workflow (support, sales, or ops).
2. Copy a prompt, fill [brackets], paste into your AI tool.
3. Log what worked in the CSV.

## Support prompts
- Rewrite this customer message to be clear and calm: [paste]
- Suggest 3 reply options: accept / delay / decline for: [request]

## Sales prompts
- Write a short WhatsApp follow-up after a quote for [offer] to [client type].
- List 5 discovery questions before pricing [service].

## Ops prompts
- Turn these steps into an SOP outline: [steps]
- Weekly review: given these numbers [paste], list 3 risks and 3 actions.

## Staff AI rules (summary)
- No customer secrets in public AI tools.
- Human checks prices, legal, and medical claims.
- Log useful prompts for the team.
