# Review & Referral Harvest Kit — instant download

Full working files also live in the repo folder `digital-products/14-review-referral-harvest-kit/` (ZIP that folder after a Paystack sale if the buyer wants the pack).

## Day-1 review ask

Hi [NAME], hope [JOB] is treating you well.
If it saved you time, could you send a 2-line review I can put on Status?
Even voice note is fine. Thank you.

## Referral ask

[NAME], if someone in your circle needs [SERVICE] before December, send them my number.
Referred clients go to the front of the queue.

## 4-line testimonial

[First name + city]
Hired us for: 
Result: 
Would rehire?

## Tracker columns

client_name, whatsapp, job, delivered_on, review_asked_on, review_received, permission_to_post, posted_on, referral_asked_on, referral_name, referral_status, thank_you_sent, notes

Import `csv/reviews-referrals.csv` into Google Sheets.
Ask only after delivery. Never invent a review. Get permission before posting a name.
