# Project & Task Tracker: quick-start guide

Your download includes **project-task-tracker.xlsx**. It works in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Numbers.

## What's inside

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| Overview | Tasks by status, overdue and due-soon counts, open and overdue work per person, and progress per project |
| Settings | Your projects, team members, statuses and priorities |
| Tasks | One row per task with owner, priority, status, dates and % done. Flags show Overdue, Due soon, On track or Done |

## Set it up

1. **Settings:** list your projects (with the client or area) and your team members. Statuses and priorities are ready.
2. Delete the example tasks and add your own.
3. Open **Overview** to see where everything stands.

## Colours on the Tasks sheet

- **Red:** past the due date and not done.
- **Amber:** due in the next 3 days.
- **Green:** done.

## How to run a project with it

1. **Break work into tasks you can finish in a day or two.** "Send homepage draft to client" is a task; "Website" is not.
2. **One owner per task.** Shared tasks drift.
3. **Every task gets a due date.** The flag says "No due date" until it has one.
4. **10-minute morning check-in:** filter Tasks by Flag = Overdue and Due soon, and agree what happens today.
5. **Weekly:** look at Overview. A person with many overdue tasks is overloaded; move work or move dates.

## Tips

- Use the filter arrows on the Tasks header to see one person or one project.
- % done accepts 0% to 100%. Overview averages it per project for a quick progress bar.
- Cells shaded light orange are for typing; the rest calculates.
- In Google Sheets: **File › Import › Upload › Replace spreadsheet**.
