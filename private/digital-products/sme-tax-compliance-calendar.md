## Quick start
1. Put VAT (~21st), PAYE (confirm your LIRS date), and pension (~7th) on your phone calendar.
2. Every month by the 5th: invoices, bank CSV, payroll, contractor bills — send to your accountant.
3. Store acknowledgement PDFs. Never rely on chat screenshots alone.

## Recurring reminders
- 7th — pension remittance
- 10th — PAYE (state LIRS; confirm)
- 14th — WHT chase
- 21st — VAT if registered
- Last Friday — receipt dump

Not legal or tax advice. Confirm dates with your accountant and current FIRS / LIRS / CAC notices.
