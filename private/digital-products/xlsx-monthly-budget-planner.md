# Monthly Budget Planner: quick-start guide

Your download includes **monthly-budget-planner.xlsx**. It opens in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Apple Numbers. Amounts are plain numbers, so it works in any currency.

## What's inside

| Sheet | What it does |
|---|---|
| Start here | Short instructions inside the file |
| Dashboard | Income, spending, money kept and savings rate for all 12 months, plus a running total |
| Settings | Your year, the month to review, your spending categories and monthly budgets, your income categories |
| Transactions | Where you log every payment in and out. Category and type are dropdowns |
| Budget | Budget vs actual for one month, per category, with amber (85–100% used) and red (over budget) warnings |

## Set it up in 10 minutes

1. **Settings:** type the year (cell B4) and the month you want to review (B5, a number from 1 to 12).
2. Replace the example budgets with your own. Rename categories so they match how you really spend. Keep the list short: 10 to 15 categories is plenty.
3. **Transactions:** delete the example rows, then log this month's payments so far.
4. Open **Budget** and **Dashboard**. Both update on their own.

## A routine that works

- **Daily (1 minute):** log what you spent today. Bank and card apps make this easy.
- **Weekly (5 minutes):** open Budget. Anything amber? Slow down in that category for the rest of the month.
- **Monthly (15 minutes):** change the month on Settings, look at the Dashboard, and adjust next month's budgets.

## Ways to use the numbers

- **Savings rate** (money kept ÷ income) is the single best number to watch. The Dashboard turns it green at 20% or more.
- **Pay yourself first:** put a fixed amount in the "Savings & investment" category at the start of the month, and log it as an expense so it can't be spent twice.
- **Irregular income** (freelancers, business owners): budget from your lowest normal month, and treat anything above it as a bonus for savings or debt.
- **Debt:** list each debt payment in "Debt repayment". As one is cleared, roll its payment into the next.

## Using Google Sheets

Go to sheets.google.com, then **File › Import › Upload**, pick the file and choose **Replace spreadsheet**. Dropdowns, formulas and colours carry over.

## Tips

- Cells shaded light orange are for typing. Other cells calculate; don't type over them.
- Need more than 30 categories or 1,000 transactions a year? Insert rows inside the existing ranges so the formulas stretch with them.
- Change the number format (Home › Number Format) if you want a currency symbol.
