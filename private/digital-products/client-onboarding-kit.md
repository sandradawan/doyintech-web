# Client Onboarding Kit
DoyinTech

## Kickoff questions
1. Business name and one-sentence offer?
2. Primary customer?
3. Goal of this project (leads, trust, bookings)?
4. Must-have pages/features?
5. Brand colours / logo links?
6. Deadline and deposit confirmed?
7. Who approves content?
8. WhatsApp number for updates?

## Welcome email
Subject: You're booked — next steps for [Project]

Hi [Name],
Thanks for the deposit. Here's what happens next:
1. You return the asset checklist (logo, copy, photos).
2. We share first draft by [date].
3. One structured feedback round.
4. Launch after balance.

Reply with anything urgent.
— DoyinTech

## Asset checklist
[ ] Logo (PNG/SVG)
[ ] Colours
[ ] Service list + prices if public
[ ] 5–10 photos
[ ] Contact + WhatsApp
[ ] Address / hours if local

## Revision rule (paste into proposal)
Two revision rounds on design direction. Extra rounds billed hourly. Content delays shift the timeline.
