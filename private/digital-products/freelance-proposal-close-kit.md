## 3. Objection scripts

**"Too expensive"**  
Happy to trim to a Starter scope at ₦[lower] — that removes [X, Y]. Full Growth stays at ₦[full] if you need [key outcome].

**"Let me think"**  
Of course. I’ll hold this quote until [date]. If priority is speed, the next open kickoff slot is [date].

**"Can you start free?"**  
I don’t do free builds. I do a free 15-minute audit so you know what to fix — paid work starts after deposit.

## 4. Follow-up sequence

**Day 0** — Proposal sent + "any questions on scope?"  
**Day 2** — Soft check-in: still relevant?  
**Day 5** — Close or release slot: "Closing this estimate Friday."
