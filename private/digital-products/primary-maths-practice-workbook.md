# Primary Maths Practice Workbook
**DoyinTech Kids · ages 5–11 · 40 worksheets with answers**

Forty printable worksheets that build up one step at a time: number bonds, adding and taking away, times tables, division, fractions, money in naira and word problems. Each sheet takes 10–15 minutes.

---

## How to use this workbook
- **Start where your child is comfortable.** If they get 18 or more right, move on. Under 12, repeat the sheet another day.
- **Little and often wins.** One sheet a day, five days a week, does more than a long session at the weekend.
- **Mark together.** Use the answer key at the back and talk through anything they got wrong.
- **Celebrate progress.** Tick off finished sheets on the tracker below.
- **Keep it fun.** Pair the sheets with the free daily challenge and times tables game at doyintech.com/kids.
